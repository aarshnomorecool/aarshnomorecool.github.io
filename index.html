Theta

A fast, private browser with ad blocking, made for every Indian phone.

  • Blocks ads and trackers on websites, so pages load faster and use less data.
  • Hides cookie pop-ups without accepting anything for you.
  • Your browsing stays on your phone. No account, no cloud history.
  • Private tabs that leave nothing behind.
  • Tab groups, tab previews and Lite mode for slow networks.
  • Works in English, हिन्दी, मराठी and Русский.
