Privacy Policy

Theta Browser for Android · Effective 8 November 2026

Theta is a web browser made by the Theta Browser team ("we"). This policy explains what Theta does with your information. The short version: your browsing stays on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your history, searches or the sites you visit.

1. What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Browsing history, open tabs, tab groups and pictures of tabs
  • Bookmarks, speed dial and saved site icons
  • Cookies, website data and cache
  • Your download list, site permissions (camera, microphone, location) and sites where you allowed ads
  • Your settings
  • Privacy stats: only a count of blocked requests and the date counting began, never which sites or requests

Android cloud backup is switched off for Theta, so this data is not copied to any cloud account.

Private tabs keep no history, and their cookies and site data are deleted when you close the last private tab.

2. Information shared with others

Websites and search engines

When you visit a website, that website (and services it uses) receive your request like in any browser, including your IP address. When you search from the address bar, your search goes to the search engine you chose in Settings. Their own privacy policies apply.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Theta is free because it shows ads. Ads appear only on Theta's own screens (such as the new-tab page, Downloads, Bookmarks and History), never inside websites. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), Theta asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Theta never sends Google or any advertiser your browsing history, searches or the sites you visit.

Crash reports and usage statistics (only if you agree)

On first launch Theta asks whether you want to share crash reports and usage statistics. This is off unless you turn it on, and you can change it any time in Settings. If you agree, Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics receive crash details and a fixed list of app events (for example "a tab was opened" or "a setting was changed"). Web addresses, site names, page titles, searches and anything you type are removed and never included.

Safe Browsing

Theta uses Google Safe Browsing, built into Android System WebView, to warn you about dangerous sites. To do this, WebView checks shortened, scrambled forms (hashes) of web addresses with Google. Google does not receive the full address of the page you are visiting.

App configuration

Theta fetches its settings for features like ad-blocking lists from Google Firebase Remote Config, and downloads ad-blocking and cookie pop-up filter lists about once a week over Wi-Fi. These requests contain no information about your browsing.

3. Permissions

Theta asks for the camera, microphone or location only when a website you are using requests them, and only after you agree for that site. You can remove these choices in Settings → Site settings. Storage access is used only to save downloads on Android 9 and older.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Settings → Clear browsing data lets you delete history, tabs, cookies and more, or everything at once.
  • Ads consent: change it in Settings → Shields and privacy → Ad privacy choices, where offered.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: turn off in Settings → Shields and privacy.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you may ask about, correct or erase personal data and raise a grievance by contacting us below. Because your browsing data is stored only on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time.

5. Children

Theta is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed at children under 13. Users under 18 should use Theta with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian.

6. Security

Theta refuses insecure certificates without exception, keeps files on your device out of reach of websites, and blocks known trackers by default. No method is completely secure, but we design Theta to keep as little data as possible.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
